Goldie Street – completed model (.glb)

This exports the finished project as one binary glTF file: the building set out to HAL AD-06/10/11, all five houses fitted out and furnished, and the street and suburb. It is in metres, Y up, with materials named by surface (concrete, oak, glass, water, render and so on). Open it in Blender, Twinmotion, Lumion, D5 Render, Enscape or SketchUp.
